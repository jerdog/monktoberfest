We do not disclose our speaker list in advance ( [here's the rationale about why](blog/why-the-monktoberfest-agenda-is-not-disclosed/)), but we recognize that it can be helpful to have a general sense of what to expect from a conference.

To that end, we have a high-level agenda [published here](/#schedule). If you'd like a further sense of content to expect, please check out some of the talks from our previous events or browse the conference recaps from the team.
